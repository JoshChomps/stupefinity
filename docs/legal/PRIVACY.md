# Stupefinity Privacy Policy

_Version 1 · Draft: review before public release_

Stupefinity runs entirely on your computer. It has no servers of its own, no accounts, no
analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your use of Stupefinity and the data below are
subject to this policy.

## What Stupefinity collects, and why
When you log in, you allow Stupefinity to access your Spotify account through Spotify's
login page. Stupefinity then uses:

- **Your login token**, to talk to Spotify on your behalf.
- **Your Spotify profile**: your user ID, display name and subscription type (to check for
  Premium).
- **Your library**: playlists, Liked Songs, albums, artists and search results, only to show
  them to you and play them.
- **Your settings**: volume, shuffle, equalizer, skip rules and similar preferences.

## Where it is kept
Everything stays on your computer, in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Stupefinity`: the login token,
settings, a lyrics cache and a log file. Playlists and library data are kept in memory only
while the app runs. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or data brokers, or sent to the
developer.

## Who Stupefinity talks to
- **Spotify**, for login, your library, search and playback.
- **LRCLIB** (lrclib.net), to look up lyrics for the song playing (the song's title, artist,
  album and length are sent).
- **Discord**, only if you turn on Rich Presence, to show the song playing on your Discord
  profile.

Those services handle data under their own privacy policies.

## Cookies
Stupefinity uses no cookies. Spotify's login page, which opens in your browser, may set its
own cookies under Spotify's policy, which you can manage in your browser's settings.

## Disconnecting and deleting your data
Click **Log Out** to disconnect your Spotify account. Stupefinity then deletes your login
token and the Spotify data it holds. You can also revoke Stupefinity's access at any time
in your Spotify account settings (Apps), and delete `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Stupefinity` to remove
everything else.
